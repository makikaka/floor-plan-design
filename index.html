Tips
  • Drag the plan to move it, scroll to zoom, double-click to fit. Click a room to edit it.
  • The drawing is seen from above: Top is the top of the screen, Left the left.
  • Starts at is measured from the wall's left corner (top and bottom walls) or top corner (left and right walls).
  • To make one room out of two rectangles (e.g. an L-shape), type the same name in Same room as for both. Their areas are added.
  • Open passage cuts a gap in the wall. Make it nearly as long as the wall to leave only a short stub.
  • Keys: F full screen, P hide or show this panel.
