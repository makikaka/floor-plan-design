Tips
  • The drawing is seen from above: Top is the top of the screen, Left the left.
  • Starts at is the distance from the wall's left corner (top and bottom walls) or top corner (left and right walls) to where the door or window begins.
  • To make one room out of two rectangles (e.g. an L-shape), type the same name in Same room as for both. Their areas are added together.
  • A door set to Open passage cuts a gap in the wall. Make it nearly as long as the wall to leave only a short stub.
  • An orange warning under the plan means rooms overlap or a door or window is longer than its wall.
